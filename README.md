# My Favorite Movies 🎬
These are some of the movies I like because they have interesting stories and different genres.

---

## 1. Project Hail Mary 🚀

**Genre:** Science Fiction, Adventure

*Project Hail Mary* is about an astronaut who wakes up alone in space with no memory of his mission. He later discovers that he has to find a way to save Earth from a mysterious threat.

> A science fiction story about space, survival, and an unexpected journey.

## 2. Death Whisperer 👻

**Genre:** Horror, Thriller, Action

*Death Whisperer* is a Thai horror movie about a young girl who becomes possessed by an evil spirit. Her brother tries to find a way to save her and protect their family.

## 3. Baahubali ⚔️

**Genre:** Action, Fantasy, Drama

*Baahubali* is an Indian movie about a young man who discovers his true identity and gets involved in a conflict over the kingdom's throne. The story focuses on family, power, and betrayal.
